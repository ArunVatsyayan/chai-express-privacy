Privacy Policy for Chai Express

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Chai Express is a free game for Android by Project Rabbit, an independent developer. It is made for players aged 9 and up, including children. This page explains what happens to your information when you play.

1. The game itself

Chai Express does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, camera or microphone. There are no ads, no purchases, and no analytics or tracking tools of our own. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.

2. The optional Chai Rush leaderboard (Google Play Games)

Chai Rush has an optional leaderboard run by Google Play Games Services. You never have to use it, and the game never signs you in by itself. Only if you tap Leaderboard and choose to sign in to Google Play Games:

  • Google Play Games links the game to your Play Games profile (your gamer name and player ID).
  • The best distance of your normal Chai Rush rides is sent to the leaderboard, where other players can see it with your gamer name. Relaxed and Challenge rides are never sent.

We do not receive your email address. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy. In the version of the game from Google Play, Google Play services may also send crash reports and diagnostic information to Google.

You can sign out, hide your activity or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings on your device or at myaccount.google.com. On children's Google accounts, a parent can manage Play Games in Google Family Link.

3. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes their child has shared personal information with us, they can contact us and we will help remove it.

4. Removing your data

To remove progress saved on your device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Leaderboard scores belong to your Google Play Games profile and can be removed there (see section 2).

5. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
